## About The Project

<img width="1280" height="675" alt="image" src="/README_Images/providence.jpg" />
<h6 align="center">Credit to GameSkinny: https://www.gameskinny.com/tips/risk-of-rain-returns-how-to-beat-providence/</h6>



Here's why:
* Reason 1: I am a really big fan of Hopoo Games. They made one of my favourite games, Risk of Rain 2.
I love the story, gameplay and visuals. However, the game itself is 3D, so I opted for the remake of the original game: Risk of Rain.
* Reason 2: I love roguelikes. The replayability factor is what drives me to keep playing these games. 
No matter how many games you play, you will never have the same run twice. 
Learning to manage limited resources within limited time is what drove me to love this series.
* Reason 3: I wanted to challenge myself and put all of my skills to the test. 
Roguelikes are known for their difficulty in development due to their gameplay. 
Items have synergies, the loot is randomly scattered throughout the map and monster spawns are correlated to your advancement.

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

To run my Game Project, you will need:
* Visual Studio 2022

(This has not been tested on any other IDE's)

### How to run the project

* Download the source code.
* Extract all and navigate to "\DAE15_Pecukevicius_Aidas"
* Open "DAE15_Pecukevicius_Aidas.sln"
* Run the project!

If you encounter problems with launching:
* Right click project "RiskOfRainReturnsRemake" and click "Build dependencies". Check if the engine is selected;
* Right click project "RiskOfRainReturnsRemake" and click "Set as Startup Project".

<!-- HOW TO PLAY -->
## How to play

The current version is unplayable.

However, later:

The goal of the game is to defeat the teleporter Boss and move onto the next stage. 
Kill Monsters and open Chests to increase your odds of survival!

The game itself is quite difficult, so don't be discouraged!
P.S. - There is fall damage.

### Controls
* Arrow Up - Look up (use to climb)
* Arrow Down - Look down (use to climb down)
* Arrow Left - Look Left
* Arrow Right - Look Right
* Z - Primary skill
* X - Secondary skill
* C - Utility skill
* V - Special skill
* S - Shoot Left
* D - Shoot Right
* G - Activate Equipment
* A - Interact
* TAB - Info Screen
